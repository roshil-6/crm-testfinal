## 🚀 Enable GitHub Pages (2 Steps)

### Step 1: Go to Settings
**Click this link:** https://github.com/roshil-6/crm-testfinal/settings/pages

### Step 2: Enable Pages
1. Under **"Source"**, select: **"GitHub Actions"**
2. Click **"Save"**

**That's it!** 🎉

---

## ⚡ What Happens Next

1. GitHub will automatically build your frontend
2. Your app will be live at: **https://roshil-6.github.io/crm-testfinal/**
3. Every time you push to `main` branch, it auto-deploys!
